# PourGrid V1 RC1.4 — Operational Inventory Units

The product editor now asks only:
- Large inventory unit
- Loose inventory unit
- Loose units per large unit

Examples:
- Tito's: Cases + Bottles, 12 per case.
- Island Oasis: Cases + Cartons, 12 per case.
- Grapefruit Juice: Cases + Cans, 24 per case.
- Fruit: Cases + Half Cases.
- CO2: Tanks only.
- BIB syrup: BIB only.

A live preview shows the conversion, such as:
4 cases + 7 bottles = 55 bottles.
